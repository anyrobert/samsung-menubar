## Samsung remote protocol

The Samsung TV remote channel, key names and `ed.*` events follow the protocol documented by community projects such as [samsungtvws](https://github.com/xchwarze/samsung-tv-ws-api) and [samsungctl](https://github.com/Ape/samsungctl). No code from those projects is included.
